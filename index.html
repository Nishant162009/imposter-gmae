            How to play

            Secret roles

            Civilians all see the same secret word. Imposters see no word, only a note (and a vague hint if hints are on).

            Passing the phone

            Hold the card to see your role, let go to hide it, then tap Continue and hand the phone over. Never look at another player's card.

            Discuss

            Each player gives one clue about the word. Imposters bluff to blend in.

            Vote and ties

            Everyone votes at once. The player with the most votes is accused. If two or more players tie, nobody is caught.

            Scoring

            Accused player is an imposter: each civilian gets 2 points. Wrong player or a tie: each imposter gets 3 points. Points are awarded once per round.

            Game modes

            Classic: balanced standard rules. Speed Run: fast discussion. Double Trouble: an extra imposter when the group size allows it. Blind Imposter: no imposter hint. Total Chaos: random category and difficulty. Hidden Partners: secret alliance roles. Fake Clue: related but different secret words. Last Chance: the accused imposter can attempt one final word guess.

            Achievements

            Play rounds to unlock badges for your first game, a 10-round streak of experience, civilian wins, imposter wins, a unanimous catch, and a big lobby. Progress stays on this device.

            Fair play & privacy

            Pass the device one player at a time. The game stores local scores and settings on this device; it does not need an account or an internet connection.

            Winning

            Civilians win by catching an imposter. Imposters win by avoiding it. In Last Chance mode, a correct final guess may change the outcome.
